My research interests include:

  • Syntactic locality: weak and strong islands, CED, intervention effects, phasality
  • Morphosyntactic analysis: e.g. negative concord, relative clauses
  • Morphosyntactic (micro)variation, especially in Romance and English dialects
  • Grammar architecture and interfaces
  • Formal and mathematical definitions of Merge
  • Efficiency trade-offs in natural language
